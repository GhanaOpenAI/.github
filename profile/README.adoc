We are currently working on research projects that improve machine translation, speech synthesis and speech recognition for African languages
Our community is made up of contributors who support several projects on a voluntary basis. We believe in community driven projects that drive innovation in natural language processing for Ghanaian languages.

You can join our community by sending a request via email - info@ghanaopenai.org.
|

=== Featured Projects

- link:https://github.com/GhanaOpenAI/ghana-corpus-builder[ghana-corpus-builder^], Streamlined access to high-quality corpora for 38 Ghanaian languages
- link:https://github.com/GhanaOpenAI/ghana-g2p[ghana-g2p^], Grapheme-to-phoneme conversion for 42 Ghanaian languages, in IPA and native orthography
- link:https://github.com/GhanaOpenAI/ghana-ipa-asr[ghana-ipa-asr^], Speech to IPA phonemes for 42 Ghanaian and West African languages, running on CPU, GPU or mobile
- link:https://github.com/GhanaOpenAI/stable-twi-tts[stable-twi-tts^], Twi and Ghanaian English speech synthesis on ONNX - fast, multi-platform and batch-capable
- link:https://github.com/GhanaOpenAI/ghana-speech-id[ghana-speech-id^], Language identification for Ghanaian and West African speech, over IPA phonemes
- link:https://github.com/GhanaOpenAI/nsanku-ASR[nsanku-ASR^], Benchmarking ASR models across 41 Ghanaian language varieties
